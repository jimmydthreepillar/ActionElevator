# Safety Testing Command Center

A clickable mockup of Action Elevator's safety-testing workflow for Testing Coordinators.

## Open it
Double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser. No internet or install needed.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages
1. In this repo, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Branch**, pick **main** and **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
3. Wait a minute. Your link will be: https://jimmydthreepillar.github.io/ActionElevator/

## Screens
1. **Cases**: open cases grouped by due month and priority, status badges, overdue QEI report alerts.
2. **Coverage**: shows if the test is covered or billable. Billable jobs stay locked until the customer's approval email is attached.
3. **Dispatch**: matches a technician by area and creates the FIELDBOSS service activities automatically.
4. **Reconcile**: shows the mechanic's result next to the QEI report and blocks closing the case when they disagree.
5. **Reinspection**: what happens when a report passes or fails, plus a tracker for fixing violations.

All customers, technicians and case numbers are sample data.
